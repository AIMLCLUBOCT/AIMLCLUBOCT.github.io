08 // INQUIRIES

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions regarding membership, research tracks, repository contribution, and prerequisites.

Who is eligible to join AIML Club OCT?

Any undergraduate student enrolled at Oriental College of Technology (OCT), Bhopal across any branch (CSE, AIML, IT, EC, ME, EX, CE) is eligible. We recruit annually for executive council roles and maintain open membership for our Discord, WhatsApp, and GitHub repositories year-round.

Are there prerequisites before joining workshop labs?

No prior machine learning experience is required. Module 01 begins with pure Python fundamentals and vector arithmetic. A basic familiarity with high school mathematics (linear equations and functions) is helpful.

How does student code get contributed and reviewed?

All students can fork our repositories, solve tagged good first issue items, and open a Pull Request. Every PR is evaluated by Tech Lead Kinshuk Verma and our system architects with automated linting and unit test verification.

Do I need a high-end GPU laptop to participate?

No. All our practical notebooks in the Workshops repository are tailored with 1-click Google Colab integrations that execute in cloud environments with free T4 GPU access.
